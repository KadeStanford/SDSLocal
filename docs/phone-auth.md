# Phone authentication status

Phone/SMS authentication is intentionally disabled for SDS Local.

The app uses email/password, email sign-in codes, Apple, and Google for account
access. Twilio is not required, configured, or billed for staging.

Business contact phone numbers remain supported as ordinary profile data. A
business can publish a phone number, and customers can tap it to call; that
feature does not send SMS and does not depend on Twilio.

Staff invitations are email-only until a phone provider is deliberately
reintroduced and funded.
